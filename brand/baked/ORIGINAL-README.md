# CAMPS.ES brand kit

A starter kit read from grimaldsociales-prog.github.io, made with [Brand Kit](https://baked.design/ai-brand-kit/) by Baked Design Studio.

| File | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| `DESIGN.md` | Colours and type, in [Google's DESIGN.md format](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md) |
| `BRAND.md` | Voice and logo use |
| `SKILL.md` | All of it in one file, as a Claude skill |
| `assets/` | Social posts and banners, pictures from your site |

## Use it with
- **Claude:** Customize, then Skills, click +, Upload a skill, and pick this zip.
- **Claude Design:** import this folder as your design system.
- **Claude Code:** copy `SKILL.md` to `.claude/skills/camps-es-brand/` in your project, and it loads on its own.
- **Cursor, Codex, Copilot:** keep `DESIGN.md` at the root of your project.
- **ChatGPT and others:** attach `DESIGN.md` and `BRAND.md`.

This kit is what can be read from the outside: your site's colours, fonts, logo and words. A full brand kit also has the rules behind them, your pictures, mascots, templates and an on-brand maker. That's what we make at https://baked.design.
